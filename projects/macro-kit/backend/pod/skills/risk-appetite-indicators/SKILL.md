# Risk-appetite indicators

- **Equity–bond correlation:** negative correlation (bonds hedge equities) suits a growth-driven regime; positive correlation signals inflation-driven markets, where duration does not hedge risk.
- **Credit spreads:** high-yield and investment-grade spreads often lead equities at turning points; watch new-issue concessions and fund flows.
- **Volatility:** equity implied vol (VIX) versus rates vol (MOVE); a divergence often resolves with the less-stressed market catching up.
- **Breadth and leadership:** cyclicals versus defensives, small versus large caps.
- **Financial conditions:** combine rates, spreads, equities and the dollar into one read of whether conditions are easing or tightening.

Output: a risk-appetite verdict (supportive / neutral / stressed), what it implies for duration and curve, and the indicator most likely to give an early warning.
