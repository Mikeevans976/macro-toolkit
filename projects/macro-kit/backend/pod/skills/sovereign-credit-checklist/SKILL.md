# Sovereign credit checklist

1. **Ratings calendar:** scheduled reviews for each agency over the horizon; one-notch moves near investment-grade thresholds or index eligibility matter most.
2. **Fiscal:** deficit path versus targets, debt dynamics (r − g), budget calendar, EU fiscal-rule procedures.
3. **Politics:** elections, coalition stability, confrontation with EU institutions.
4. **Central bank backstop:** ECB reinvestment flexibility, TPI eligibility conditions, QT pace and its distribution by country.
5. **Supply:** gross and net issuance versus last year, syndications, buybacks (see `supply-playbook`).
6. **Investor base:** domestic versus foreign holders, bank holdings, retail programmes.
7. **Index events:** month-end extension, index inclusion or exclusion.

Mark each item and say which ones the current spread already reflects.
