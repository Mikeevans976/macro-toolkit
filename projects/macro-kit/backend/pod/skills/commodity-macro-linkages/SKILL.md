# Commodity-macro linkages

- **Oil:** headline inflation via fuel within one to two months; second-round effects via transport and energy-intensive goods later. Check OPEC+ decisions, inventories and the futures curve (backwardation = tight market).
- **Natural gas and power (Europe especially):** utility tariffs, with lags set by regulators; large effect on euro-area and UK inflation.
- **Metals:** copper and iron ore track China's credit cycle and global manufacturing.
- **Agriculture:** food inflation with lags of several months; weather and export restrictions.
- **FX links:** commodity currencies (CAD, NOK, AUD, NZD) and terms of trade.

Output: the commodity moves that matter for the pod's inflation and FX views, with the expected lag and size of pass-through.
