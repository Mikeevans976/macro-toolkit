# IC memo template

Use this structure when you synthesise your team's work.

## 1. Bottom line (three lines at most)
The single most important conclusion and what the pod should do about it.

## 2. Regime and macro backdrop
Where we are in the cycle and what the central banks will do next, citing the economists' and desks' views.

## 3. Ranked ideas
A table:

| # | Idea | Instrument | Conviction | Horizon | Catalyst | Invalidation | Red Team verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Rank by conviction × asymmetry, not by how interesting an idea is. At most five ideas.

## 4. Where the team disagrees
Name the disagreement, both sides, and your call. Don't average it away.

## 5. Risks to the book
What hurts the ideas *together*: shared factor exposure, a common catalyst, crowding.

## 6. Data caveats
Anything simulated, stale or missing that weakens the conclusions.

## Rules
- Only include ideas that came from your team's work this run, or from views recorded in `read_desk_views`.
- If a Red Team verdict is `reject`, either drop the idea or say explicitly why you overrule it.
- Recommendations are advisory. The human PM decides.
