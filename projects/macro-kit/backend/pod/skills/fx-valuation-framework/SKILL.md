# FX valuation framework

- **Rate differentials:** the main short-term driver for G10 pairs. Compare the pair's move with the change in 2y differentials; a gap often closes.
- **Real effective exchange rates and PPP:** long-run anchors (BIS REER). They matter at extremes and over years, rarely within months.
- **Behavioural models (BEER):** regress the currency on terms of trade, productivity, net foreign assets and real rates; the residual is the mispricing.
- **Carry:** the forward points; carry-to-vol ranks how much protection carry gives against moves.
- **Flows and positioning:** CFTC positioning, hedging flows, M&A, reserve managers.
- **Regime:** in risk-off regimes, safe havens (USD, JPY, CHF) dominate valuation.

Output: per pair, the driver that matters now, the gap to the driver-implied level, carry, and the regime caveat.
