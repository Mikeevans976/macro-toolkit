# House style for publications

## Morning note
1. **Headline:** one sentence with the day's main call.
2. **Overnight and today:** three to five bullets: what moved, what's coming (data, auctions, speakers).
3. **House view:** direction, curve, spreads, inflation, FX, one line each.
4. **Ideas:** the ledger's ideas with status and conviction (from `list_trades`).
5. **Risks:** the scenario the book is most exposed to.
6. **Data caveats.**

## Rules
- Numbers with units; dates in full; no unexplained jargon.
- One page. Link to longer analysis rather than including it.
- Publish with `publish_report` (kind 'morning-note'). Distribution to people is done by the PM.
