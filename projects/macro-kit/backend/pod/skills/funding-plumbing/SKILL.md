# Funding plumbing

- **Reserves and central bank balance sheets:** QT reduces reserves; watch for the point where reserves stop being abundant (repo rates trading above the policy floor, rising use of standing facilities).
- **US specifics:** Treasury General Account swings and the reverse-repo facility move reserves; heavy bill issuance drains liquidity.
- **Repo:** general collateral versus specials; bonds trading special are richer in cash than their swap-equivalents imply.
- **Quarter-end and year-end:** bank balance-sheet constraints raise funding rates and widen basis around reporting dates.
- **Cross-currency basis:** dollar funding demand shows up in EURUSD and USDJPY basis; widening signals stress.
- **Swap spreads:** affected by balance-sheet costs, repo, and supply.

Output: the current state (abundant / tightening / scarce), the next pressure date, and its effect on swap spreads, basis and front-end rates.
