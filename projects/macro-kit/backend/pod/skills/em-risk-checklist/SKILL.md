# EM risk checklist

Go through each item and mark it green, amber or red.

1. **External balance:** current account, basic balance, reliance on portfolio flows.
2. **Reserves:** months of imports, versus short-term external debt.
3. **Inflation and policy credibility:** real policy rate, central bank independence, inflation expectations.
4. **Fiscal:** primary balance, debt trajectory, share of FX-denominated debt, upcoming refinancing.
5. **Politics:** elections, institutional risk, sanctions exposure.
6. **Positioning and liquidity:** foreign ownership of local bonds, carry-trade crowding, market depth in stress.
7. **Global drivers:** sensitivity to US rates, the dollar, commodity prices and China.
8. **Contagion channels:** shared investor base, regional peers, index inclusion events.

## Output
A one-line verdict per country (carry worth the risk / not), the red items, and the global scenario that would hurt the position most.
