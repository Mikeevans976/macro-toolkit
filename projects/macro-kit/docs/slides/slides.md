## 2. Tool's end-goal

## 3. Some more details on how we look at point 2.2 above and future areas for build-up
1. Themes and trades are analysed through the lens of risk/reward: what is the premium or upfront cost of the trade, what is the risk/reward or payout/cost ratio across all trades, what the estimated PnL of the portfolio is, using. a probability-weighted approach. 
2. We will create an AI agent which will be our portfolio builder: will use its tools (the toolkit we created here, access to the portfolio, access to sell-side research,...) in order to carry out 2.2 above.
